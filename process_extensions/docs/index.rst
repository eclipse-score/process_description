AI-Supported Process Execution
===============================

These draft documents explain how humans and AI tools can work together within
the existing process. They do not change its responsibilities or approval rules.

For the Change Management Plan pilot, start with
:doc:`technical_workflows/examples/platform_management_plan`. It uses two
technical workflows: editing a plan and reviewing its pull request.

The concept explains the reasoning behind the approach. The requirements
inspection guidance and demonstrator describe a separate example; they are not
needed to edit or review a platform management plan.
